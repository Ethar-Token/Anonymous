Privacy Policy

We believe that people should have the freedom to express themselves while understanding how their information is collected, used, stored and protected. This policy explains our approach to privacy when you use Anonymous.

Last updated: November 10, 2026 Applies worldwide Privacy and data protection

Welcome to Anonymous ("Anonymous", "we", "our" or "us"). We are committed to respecting the privacy of people who visit, access or participate in our platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use anonymous.ethartoken.com , how that information may be used, when it may be shared, how long it may be retained and what choices and rights may be available to you.

Anonymous is designed to allow people to share confessions, thoughts, experiences and opinions without publicly displaying their identity. However, anonymous participation is not the same as guaranteed anonymity or complete confidentiality.

Please read this policy carefully. By accessing or using the platform, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to review this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular form of processing, we will seek that consent separately.

01

Scope of This Privacy Policy

This policy applies to information processed in connection with your use of Anonymous, including when you:

  • Visit or browse our website.
  • Submit anonymous confessions, stories, thoughts or other content.
  • Comment on or interact with community submissions.
  • React to, vote on or otherwise engage with content.
  • Report content that may violate our Community Rules.
  • Contact us with questions, complaints or privacy requests.
  • Interact with features, technologies or third-party services integrated into the platform.

This policy does not automatically apply to websites, services or applications operated independently by third parties, even when they are linked from Anonymous.

02

Information We Collect

The information we process depends on how you use Anonymous and which features are available. We aim to collect and process information that is reasonably necessary for operating, protecting and improving the platform.

A. Information you voluntarily provide

When you interact with the platform, you may provide information such as:

  • Anonymous confessions, stories, opinions and other submissions.
  • Comments, replies, reactions and other interactions.
  • Information included in reports or complaints.
  • Messages and correspondence sent to our support or contact channels.
  • Any personal information you voluntarily include in submitted content.

You are responsible for considering what information you include in your submissions. You should avoid including personal details that could identify you or another person unless you have a legitimate reason and are comfortable sharing them.

B. Information collected automatically

When you access a website, certain technical information may be processed automatically by the website, its hosting infrastructure or integrated services. Depending on the services in use, this may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device type, operating system and technical characteristics.
  • Approximate geographic location derived from an IP address.
  • Pages visited, referring pages and interaction information.
  • Dates, times and duration of website requests or visits.
  • Technical logs, error reports and security-related events.

C. Information from service providers

Some information may be processed or made available to us through providers that support the operation of the platform, such as hosting, security, analytics, advertising or other technical services, where those services are integrated.

The information involved depends on the provider, the configuration of its services and your interaction with the relevant features.

03

Anonymous Posts and User-Generated Content

User submissions are central to Anonymous. We process the content people submit so that we can operate the community and make its features available.

Submitted information may include confessions, stories, comments, opinions, reactions, reports and other interactions supported by the platform.

Public visibility of submissions

Content submitted for public display may be visible to other visitors and users of Anonymous. Depending on the platform's features and configuration, public content may also be accessed through search engines, external links, screenshots, sharing or other websites.

Once content becomes publicly available, we cannot control every subsequent copy, screenshot, quotation or redistribution made by third parties.

Anonymous does not mean untraceable

Our anonymous posting experience is intended to avoid publicly displaying the identity of the person who submits a confession or other anonymous content.

However, technical information such as IP addresses, server logs or other diagnostic information may be processed by the platform or its infrastructure providers, depending on how the service is configured.

We therefore cannot promise that every submission is technically untraceable or that no identifying information is ever processed.

Information about other people

Do not submit another person's private information, intimate material or identifying details without an appropriate legal basis or authorization. You must follow our Community Rules when publishing content.

Removal of submissions

Where supported by our platform and subject to applicable legal obligations, we may remove or restrict access to submitted content following an appropriate request or moderation decision.

Removal from Anonymous does not necessarily remove copies already stored by search engines, archives, other websites or people who previously accessed or shared the content.

When you visit a website, your browser communicates with servers and other technical systems to request and display information.

During these communications, technical data may be processed to deliver website content, maintain availability, identify errors and protect the platform from misuse.

This may include:

  • IP addresses and request metadata.
  • Browser and device information.

  • Information required to diagnose technical problems.

Hosting, content delivery network and security providers may independently process technical information in accordance with their own services and applicable contractual arrangements.

Some technical information may be retained in operational or security logs even when a user does not create an account or provide a name.

Legal Basis for Processing Personal Information

Where applicable data protection law requires a lawful basis for processing personal information, we will rely on an appropriate basis depending on the circumstances.

These may include:

  • Consent: Where you have freely given permission for a specific processing activity that requires consent.
  • Legitimate interests: Where processing is reasonably necessary for legitimate purposes, such as maintaining security, preventing abuse and improving our services, provided your rights and interests do not override those interests.
  • Performance of a contract: Where processing is necessary to provide a service or fulfill an agreement with you, where applicable.
  • Legal obligations: Where processing is necessary to comply with applicable laws or valid legal requirements.
  • Vital interests: Where processing is necessary to protect someone's life or physical safety in circumstances recognized by applicable law.

The lawful basis will depend on the specific processing activity. We do not assume that one legal basis applies to every category of information.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent subject to applicable law. Withdrawal does not invalidate processing that took place lawfully before the withdrawal.

Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and the applicable data protection laws, you may have certain rights concerning your personal information.

These rights may include:

  • Right of access: Request confirmation of whether we process your personal information and obtain access to information about that processing.
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of personal information in circumstances where applicable law provides this right.
  • Right to restrict processing: Request that certain processing activities be restricted where legally available.
  • Right to object: Object to certain processing activities, including processing based on legitimate interests where applicable.
  • Right to data portability: Request a copy of certain information in a commonly used, machine-readable format where this right applies.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent for processing activities that rely on your consent.
  • Right to complain: Lodge a complaint with an appropriate data protection authority where available.

Exercising your rights

You may submit a privacy request through the contact details provided in this policy.

We may need to request reasonable information to verify your identity or establish that you are authorized to make the request.

We will assess and respond to valid requests within the timeframes required by applicable law.

Important considerations for anonymous submissions

Because Anonymous is designed for anonymous participation, we may not always be able to identify which public submission belongs to a particular person.

If you wish to request access to, correction of or deletion of a particular submission, providing the relevant post link or identifier may help us locate it.

We may be unable to verify ownership of a submission based solely on a claim that someone created it. Any verification process will be handled with consideration for privacy and applicable law.

Some requests may be limited where an exception or lawful retention obligation applies.
