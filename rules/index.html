Community Rules & Guidelines

A space to speak freely, share honestly and express yourself without judgment. These guidelines help keep Anonymous safe, respectful and welcoming for everyone.

Last updated: November 10, 2026 Applies worldwide Public community policy

Anonymous exists to give people a place to express thoughts, share experiences, reveal secrets, tell stories and discuss things they may not feel comfortable sharing elsewhere.

We believe that people should be able to express themselves openly, including about difficult, controversial, embarrassing or sensitive experiences. We do not expect every member of the community to agree with one another.

However, freedom of expression comes with responsibility. Anonymity must never become an excuse for abuse, exploitation, threats, harassment or harm to others.

These Community Rules establish the standards that apply to everyone who uses Anonymous, whether they submit a confession, comment on a post, react to content or otherwise participate in the community.

01

Respect, Harassment and Bullying

Disagreement, criticism, humor and honest opinions are welcome. Targeted abuse and intimidation are not.

Do not use Anonymous to:

  • Harass, bully, intimidate or repeatedly target another person.
  • Publish content intended to humiliate, degrade or emotionally abuse someone.
  • Encourage others to attack, threaten, shame or harass an individual.
  • Organize coordinated harassment campaigns or pile-ons.
  • Make repeated unwanted sexual, threatening or abusive remarks about someone.
  • Target individuals with malicious rumors, degrading personal attacks or deliberate humiliation.

You may share negative experiences, describe conflicts, criticize behavior and express frustration. However, content should focus on expressing an experience or opinion rather than mobilizing abuse against an identifiable individual.

Our standard: You can disagree with someone, criticize their actions or tell an uncomfortable story. You cannot use the platform to turn that person into a target for abuse.
02

Hate Speech and Discrimination

Anonymous welcomes people from different countries, cultures, backgrounds and walks of life.

We do not permit content that promotes hatred, dehumanization, exclusion, persecution or violence against people based on protected or personal characteristics.

Prohibited content includes:

  • Slurs or degrading attacks directed at people because of their race, ethnicity, nationality or origin.
  • Attacks based on religion, caste, sex, gender, sexual orientation or disability.
  • Content portraying a protected group as inherently inferior, dangerous or undeserving of basic rights.
  • Calls for discrimination, exclusion, persecution or violence against a group of people.
  • Promotion or celebration of hate-based violence or extremist persecution.

Discussing discrimination, sharing personal experiences of prejudice, debating social issues and criticizing beliefs or institutions are permitted, provided the content does not cross into prohibited hateful conduct.

03

Privacy, Personal Information and Doxxing

Privacy is central to the purpose of Anonymous. We expect users to respect their own privacy and the privacy of other people.

Do not publish, request, distribute or threaten to expose another person's private or identifying information without their authorization.

This includes:

  • Home addresses, private phone numbers and personal email addresses.
  • Government identification numbers, financial information or private account credentials.
  • Precise real-time locations or information that could expose someone's whereabouts.
  • Private photographs, videos, messages or correspondence shared without authorization.
  • Information about someone's family members, workplace, school or other private associations when used to identify, locate or endanger them.
  • Information deliberately combined to reveal the identity of an anonymous or pseudonymous person.

You must also avoid publishing information that could reasonably allow someone to identify a private person, even if you do not explicitly include their name.

Important: Never post your own passwords, financial credentials, identification documents or sensitive personal information. Being anonymous on the platform does not make information you voluntarily publish safe from being copied or shared.
04

Threats, Violence and Dangerous Conduct

We do not allow content that threatens, encourages, celebrates or facilitates violence against individuals or groups.

Prohibited content includes:

  • Credible threats to kill, injure, assault or physically harm someone.
  • Encouragement or instructions to carry out violent attacks.
  • Content celebrating real-world acts of violence against victims.
  • Threats intended to frighten, intimidate or control another person.
  • Instructions that meaningfully facilitate violent wrongdoing.
  • Content encouraging vigilantism or attacks against individuals accused of wrongdoing.

Fictional storytelling, discussions of historical events, news commentary and personal accounts involving violence may be permitted when they do not encourage or facilitate real-world harm.

05

Sexual Content and Exploitation

Anonymous may be used to discuss relationships, intimacy, sexual experiences, personal boundaries and sensitive adult topics. Such discussions must remain respectful and lawful.

We do not permit:

  • Non-consensual intimate images, videos or sexual recordings.
  • Sexual exploitation, coercion or blackmail.
  • Sexual threats or content intended to pressure someone into sexual activity.
  • Content advertising or facilitating sexual exploitation or trafficking.
  • Sexual content involving minors or depicting the sexual exploitation of children.
  • Requests for, distribution of or links to child sexual abuse material.

Sharing an intimate image or private sexual information without the subject's consent is prohibited, even if the material was originally obtained consensually.

06

Child Safety and Protection of Minors

Protecting children from exploitation and abuse is a fundamental platform priority.

Anonymous has zero tolerance for child sexual exploitation or content that sexualizes, endangers or facilitates the abuse of minors.

Strictly prohibited content includes:

  • Child sexual abuse material in any form.
  • Sexualized images, descriptions or depictions of minors.
  • Sexual solicitation, grooming or attempts to exploit children.
  • Requests for sexual images or intimate information from minors.
  • Instructions, encouragement or arrangements facilitating child exploitation.
  • Attempts to identify, locate, contact or endanger a child for abusive purposes.
Content involving suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation may be removed and escalated to appropriate authorities or relevant reporting organizations where legally required or appropriate. Do not download, redistribute or circulate suspected illegal material.

Never use Anonymous to arrange private contact with minors for sexual, exploitative or otherwise harmful purposes.

07

Self-Harm, Suicide and Dangerous Content

People should be able to talk openly about emotional struggles, mental health challenges, trauma and difficult personal experiences.

We do not permit content that encourages, glorifies or provides instructions for self-harm, suicide or dangerous behavior.

  • Encouraging another person to attempt suicide or harm themselves.
  • Providing instructions, methods or practical assistance for self-harm or suicide.
  • Encouraging eating disorders, dangerous starvation or other seriously harmful behavior.
  • Promoting dangerous challenges intended to cause injury or death.
  • Pressuring or manipulating vulnerable individuals into harming themselves.

Personal recovery stories, discussions of mental health, expressions of distress and requests for emotional support are not automatically violations.

Anonymous is a community platform, not an emergency response service or a substitute for professional medical or mental health care. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis support service.
08

Illegal Activities and Harmful Instructions

Do not use Anonymous to plan, promote, facilitate, recruit for or profit from criminal activity or other serious wrongdoing.

Prohibited content includes:

  • Offers to sell illegal goods, stolen property or prohibited services.
  • Instructions facilitating serious criminal activity.
  • Fraud, scams, phishing and financial deception.
  • Recruitment or coordination for criminal operations.
  • Promotion of human trafficking, exploitation or forced labor.
  • Attempts to obtain passwords, financial credentials or other sensitive information through deception.
  • Content that meaningfully facilitates evading law enforcement while committing wrongdoing.

Discussions of crime, confessions about past wrongdoing, fictional criminal stories, journalism and educational discussions are not automatically prohibited. However, they must not become instructions, offers or arrangements to commit crimes.

09

Deception, Impersonation and Misleading Content

Anonymous is a place for personal expression, including opinions, stories and subjective experiences. Not every post needs to be independently verified or objectively established as fact.

However, users must not:

  • Impersonate another person, organization, public authority or company to deceive others.
  • Publish fabricated emergency alerts or false threats intended to cause panic or harm.
  • Use fake claims to solicit money, credentials or private information.
  • Deliberately manipulate the community through coordinated deceptive activity.
  • Present fraudulent offers, fabricated endorsements or false guarantees.

You are responsible for the content you submit. Do not present allegations about identifiable people as established facts when you have no reasonable basis for doing so.

10

Spam, Advertising and Platform Manipulation

We want the community to be about genuine human expression rather than repetitive advertising, artificial engagement or commercial manipulation.

Prohibited behavior includes:

  • Repeatedly posting identical or substantially similar content.
  • Flooding the platform with irrelevant submissions or comments.
  • Posting unsolicited advertisements or promotional campaigns.
  • Using automated tools, scripts or bots to manipulate engagement.
  • Artificially inflating reactions, votes or popularity.
  • Posting deceptive links, phishing pages or malicious downloads.
  • Manipulating discussions through coordinated fake engagement.

Genuine participation and relevant sharing are welcome. Commercial promotion must not disrupt the community or deceive users.

11

Copyright and Intellectual Property

Respect the intellectual property and creative rights of others.

  • Do not submit copyrighted material in a way that infringes another person's rights.
  • Do not claim ownership of another person's original work.
  • Do not distribute protected material without the necessary authorization or legal basis.
  • Do not use Anonymous to distribute stolen, pirated or unauthorized material.

Users should submit content they created, have permission to share or are otherwise legally entitled to publish.

If you believe material on Anonymous infringes your copyright or other intellectual property rights, contact us through the appropriate reporting or contact channel.

12

Posting Responsibly

Before submitting a confession, story or comment, take a moment to consider its potential impact.

We encourage users to:

  • Think carefully before sharing information about other people.
  • Remove names and identifying details when they are unnecessary.
  • Avoid including private conversations or sensitive information without permission.
  • Distinguish personal opinions and experiences from verified facts.
  • Use respectful language, even when discussing difficult or controversial subjects.
  • Consider whether a post could expose someone to harassment, discrimination or real-world danger.

You do not need to share your real name or identity to participate. You should, however, take responsibility for the material you choose to publish.

13

Content Moderation and Enforcement

Anonymous reserves the right to review, restrict or remove content that violates these Community Rules or creates a significant safety, legal or operational risk for the platform or its users.

Depending on the nature and seriousness of a violation, possible enforcement actions include:

  • Removing content that violates these rules.
  • Restricting the visibility or distribution of content.
  • Disabling access to particular platform features where available.
  • Blocking or restricting repeat abusive submissions where technically possible.
  • Removing content that presents a serious risk to individuals or the wider community.
  • Preserving or disclosing relevant information where required by applicable law.
  • Reporting suspected illegal activity to appropriate authorities or relevant organizations where legally required or appropriate.

Enforcement decisions may take into account the severity of the violation, the context, the potential for harm, repeated behavior and applicable legal obligations.

Serious violations

Certain violations, including child sexual exploitation, credible threats of violence, doxxing and serious exploitation, may result in immediate removal and additional action without prior warning.

No guarantee of advance notice

We are not required to issue a warning before removing content or taking other appropriate protective action. Where appropriate and feasible, we may provide an explanation of the action taken.

Moderation decisions are made to protect the community and maintain the integrity of the platform. Compliance with these rules does not guarantee that content will remain available indefinitely.
14

Reporting Content and Violations

Every member of the community can help make Anonymous safer by reporting content that violates these rules.

If you encounter a confession, comment or other content that appears abusive, dangerous, exploitative or otherwise prohibited, use the report option available on the platform where provided.

When submitting a report:

  • Identify the content you believe violates the rules.
  • Select the most relevant reason for your report, where available.
  • Provide a clear explanation when additional details are requested.
  • Do not submit knowingly false or malicious reports.

Reports may be reviewed and assessed in accordance with the seriousness of the issue and the platform's available moderation resources.

Reporting content does not guarantee its removal. However, reports help us identify potential violations and assess risks to the community.

For emergencies: Do not rely on the platform's reporting feature to obtain emergency assistance. Contact the appropriate emergency services in your location if someone faces immediate danger.
15

Important Limitations of Anonymity

Anonymous provides a platform for anonymous expression. However, anonymity should not be interpreted as a guarantee of absolute privacy, confidentiality or untraceability.

Once published, content may be viewed, copied, photographed, shared or preserved by other people. Removing a post does not guarantee that all copies elsewhere on the internet will disappear.

We also cannot guarantee that other users will not recognize details in a story or connect information to the person who submitted it.

Users should avoid including details that could personally identify themselves or another person unless they have carefully considered the consequences.

Anonymous may take appropriate action in response to valid legal requests and other obligations under applicable law. The handling of personal information is explained separately in our Privacy Policy.

16

Changes to These Community Rules

We may update these Community Rules from time to time to reflect changes in the platform, community needs, safety standards, technology or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with a revised last-updated date.

We encourage users to review these rules periodically to remain informed about the standards governing participation in Anonymous.

Our commitment: We want Anonymous to remain a place where people can speak openly, share honestly and participate without fear of harassment, exploitation or abuse.
